## The habits that matter most

1. **Code a little every day.** 30-60 minutes daily beats one 5-hour session a week. Your brain builds skills during sleep, so spacing helps
2. **Type everything yourself.** Copy-pasting teaches nothing. Even when you follow a tutorial, type the code and change something to see what happens
3. **Predict, then run.** Before you run code, say what it will print. When you are wrong, you have found a gap in your mental model, which is the most valuable thing a mistake can show you
4. **Build projects early.** Do not wait until you "know enough". Small projects (a quiz, a calculator, a to-do list) teach what lessons cannot: how the pieces fit together
5. **Struggle first, then look.** Spend 15-20 minutes trying before you ask for help or read a solution. The struggle is what makes the answer stick

---

## Avoid "tutorial hell"

Watching tutorial after tutorial feels productive but builds little skill. Use the **70/30 rule**: spend about 30% of your time learning and 70% **building and practicing**.

Good sign: you close the tutorial and build a variation of it from scratch.

## Make progress visible

- Keep a notes file of things you learned and mistakes you made
- Keep a project list and finish small things. A finished tiny project beats an abandoned big one
- Re-read your old code after a month. You will see how much you improved (and what to refactor)

## When you are stuck

- **Explain the problem out loud** (the rubber duck technique). Often you spot the answer while explaining
- Shrink the problem: make the smallest example that still fails
- Read the error message slowly, word by word
- Take a break. Many bugs are solved in the shower
- When you ask others, show your code, the exact error, what you expected and what you already tried

## Roadmap

1. Basics: variables, conditions, loops, functions
2. Data: lists, maps/dictionaries, strings, files
3. Structure: classes/structs, modules, error handling
4. Tools: Git, the terminal, a debugger
5. Projects, then algorithms and data structures
6. Specialize: web, mobile, games, data, systems
